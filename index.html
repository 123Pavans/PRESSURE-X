Scouting Intelligence Architecture

PRESSURE-X employs a dual multi-factor percentile framework specifically configured to bypass raw aggregate volume bias. By isolating true domestic match efficiency across Syed Mushtaq Ali Trophy (SMAT) seasons, the engine prioritizes players demonstrating superior phase discipline, scoring consistency, and tactical versatility under competitive domestic constraints.

Emerging Criteria Filter

Restricted to domestic talents age ≤ 29 with minimum thresholds of 8 matches & 250 runs (batters) or 8 matches & 12 wickets (bowlers).

Phase-Specialized Bowling

Every candidate ball bowled categorized into Powerplay (0–5), Middle (6–14), and Death (15–19) to isolate defensive economy vs wicket-taking phase threats.

Rigorous Data Integrity

Zero statistical fabrication. Names, stats, and phase metrics strictly reflect authentic domestic ball-by-ball outputs with transparent limitation disclosures.

Quantitative Framework

Scouting Scoring Methodology

Detailed mathematical formulas, percentile models, weighting distributions, and boundary constraints.

Batter Scoring Model (100 pts)

Designed to balance raw run conversion rate with tempo and standard deviation consistency across seasons.

  • Average Percentile 25%
  • Strike Rate Percentile 25%
  • Total Runs Percentile 20%
  • Scoring Consistency (SR SD inv.) 15%
  • Recent Matches Density 10%
  • Active Seasons Longevity 5%
Score_Batter = 0.25*P(Avg) + 0.25*P(SR) + 0.20*P(Runs) + 0.15*P(Cons) + 0.10*P(Rec) + 0.05*P(Exp)
Bowler Scoring Model (100 pts)

Optimized to identify defensive match winners and lethal strike bowlers across tight domestic conditions.

  • Bowling Average Percentile 25%
  • Economy Rate Percentile 25%
  • Total Wickets Percentile 20%
  • Bowling Strike Rate 15%
  • Economy Consistency (Econ SD inv.) 10%
  • Recent Matches Density 5%
Score_Bowler = 0.25*P(Avg) + 0.25*P(Econ) + 0.20*P(Wkts) + 0.15*P(SR) + 0.10*P(Cons) + 0.05*P(Rec)

Candidate Filtering & Eligibility Rules

1. Batting Qualification Threshold

Must have played at least 8 domestic T20 matches and scored a minimum of 250 career runs in the SMAT database.

2. Bowling Qualification Threshold

Must have bowled in at least 8 domestic T20 matches and claimed a minimum of 12 wickets.

3. Age Demographics Filter

Candidates filtered at Age ≤ 29 as a supporting talent identification criterion to spotlight prime recruitment years for IPL 2027.

Official Model Limitations & Integrity Disclosures
  • Data Archive Horizon: Historical domestic SMAT delivery archive terminates in the 2024/25 season. 2025/26 form is not captured in this model.
  • IPL Career Audit: The identity match audit covers available tournament data from 2022–2026. Name matching limitations mean "No appearance found" is not proof of zero career exposure.
  • Relative Percentiles: Percentile distributions are calculated strictly relative to the filtered candidate pool, not all historical cricket participants.
  • Sample Variance: Small sample sizes (e.g. 9–11 matches) can create upward or downward statistical sensitivity in individual rankings.
  • Auction Verification: The model does not independently establish player availability, NOC status, or BCCI auction registration.
  • Team Suitability: Statistical dominance does not automatically guarantee role chemistry; franchise trials and video assessments remain imperative.
  • Non-Affiliation: PRESSURE-X is an independent sports analytics evaluation framework and does not claim official commercial endorsement by Rajasthan Royals.
